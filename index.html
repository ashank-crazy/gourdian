    Does the model work?

    The model is trained on your oldest 70% of sessions, then scored minute by minute on the newest 30%, which it has never seen.

    Each dot is a fifth of the test minutes: predicted risk (across) against the share that really slipped (up). Near the line means well calibrated.

    Do Jack's warnings work?

    When the model rates the next 5 minutes as risky, Jack warns you half the time, picked at random. The other half is the control group. This is a micro-randomized trial: comparing the two measures what the warning itself does, not just a correlation.

    The data

    Gourdian keeps a minute-level log of each session in this browser: when it started, when you left the tab, for how long, and where you said you went. No account, no server, no analytics scripts. Download it, or delete it from the Stats tab.
